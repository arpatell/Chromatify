# Chromatify SEO Notes

## Product Summary
Chromatify is a Spotify music aura generator that maps listening history to a personalized color identity.

## Search Terms
- Chromatify
- music aura generator
- Spotify aura app
- social music sharing
- song color palette
- music mood colors
- top songs aura

## Messaging Pillars
- Turn Spotify listening into a shareable aura color palette.
- Compare music mood across Last Month, Last 6 Months, and All Time.
- Export a visual aura card with songs and artwork for social posting.

## Metadata Placement
- Primary metadata: `public/index.html`
- Social previews: Open Graph + Twitter tags
- Structured data: JSON-LD `WebApplication`
